Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you really answer calls 24/7?

Yes. Emergency dispatch is staffed around the clock, every day of the year, including holidays. After-hours emergency rates apply outside our standard business hours.

How fast can someone get to my house?

Most Loveland and Fort Collins calls get a same-day appointment window, and true emergencies (active leaks, no heat in winter) are prioritized first — typically within 1–2 hours during the day.

Is the quote really free, and is the price locked in?

The on-site quote is free with no obligation. Once you approve the flat price, that's what you pay for the described job — it won't change unless the scope of work changes.

What areas do you serve?

We run daily routes through Loveland, Fort Collins, Windsor, Berthoud, and Timnath. If you're just outside these towns, call the office — we take exceptions for emergencies when a truck is nearby.

Do you work on older homes with outdated plumbing?

Regularly. Northern Colorado has plenty of homes with galvanized pipe or aging polybutylene, and our repiping estimates always account for what's actually behind the wall, not just a generic average.
