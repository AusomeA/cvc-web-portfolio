Who’s baking

About the Bakers

Mara Jansen and Theo Whitfield met in a restaurant kitchen and bonded over a shared opinion: most bread is baked for shelf life, not flavor. In 2019 they opened Juniper & Rye out of a converted garage on Mulberry Fields Lane with one mixer, a secondhand deck oven, and a sourdough starter named Clementine (still going strong).

Mara runs the ovens and keeps the bread program honest — long ferments, stone-milled flour, nothing rushed. Theo trained in pastry in Denver and handles cakes, laminated dough, and the coffee bar. Between them they’ve got about 25 years of kitchen scars and one very opinionated cat who supervises from the front window.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far ahead should I order a custom cake?

We recommend two weeks for weddings and larger events, and 72 hours minimum for birthday or small celebration cakes, depending on our schedule.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

We offer a rotating gluten-free loaf on Saturdays and can adapt most cake flavors to be dairy-free or vegan with advance notice — just mention it in your cake request.

Is there parking?

Yes — a small lot behind the building off Rye Street, plus free street parking along Mulberry Fields Lane after 9am.

Do you cater events?

We do pastry boxes, bread baskets, and coffee service for events up to about 80 guests. Reach out at least a week ahead.

Can I call ahead to reserve bread?

Yes, please do — our specialty loaves sell out most mornings. Call (970) 555-0148 and we'll set a loaf aside with your name on it.
