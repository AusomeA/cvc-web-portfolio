Two bakers and a secondhand deck oven

Mara Jansen and Theo Whitfield met on the line of a Denver restaurant kitchen and agreed on one thing: most bread is baked to last on a shelf, not to taste good the same morning.

In 2019 they opened Juniper & Rye in a converted garage on Millstone Row with one spiral mixer, a secondhand deck oven and a sourdough starter called Clementine, who still does the heavy lifting.

Mara runs the bread: long cold ferments, stone-milled flour, nothing rushed. Theo does the laminated dough, the cakes and the coffee bar. The oven is lit at 4:00 every morning we're open.

I plan my Saturday mornings around the nine o'clock focaccia.

K.M., a regular since 2020 (sample review)

Do you have gluten-free bread?

A buckwheat and oat loaf on Saturdays. It's baked in the same oven as everything else, so it isn't safe for celiac disease.

Can cakes be dairy-free or vegan?

Most sponges can. Mention it in your cake request and Theo will tell you which fillings work.

Do you cater?

Pastry boxes, bread baskets and coffee for up to about 80 people. Give us a week's notice.
