Skip Stop Pictures, its four films (The Last Local, Delancey After Midnight, Fire Escape Summer, Water Towers),
their taglines, synopses and credits, and every person named on the site and the posters are made up. The posters
and merch photos were composed for this sample from the photos above. The photos are real New York scenes, so real
street and subway signs appear in some of them; no real film, company or person is depicted.
The address (Example Ave), the 555 phone number and the .example email addresses are not real.
