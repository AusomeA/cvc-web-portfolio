The Last Local

One conductor, one last run, every stop to the end of the line.

The night the line is cut, a conductor works the last local out of Chambers Street. Between midnight and dawn, eleven stops and a handful of regulars change what she thinks she is leaving behind.

About the studio

Skip Stop Pictures is a small film and television company in Long Island City, Queens. Producer Nell Pruitt and director Marisol Treadwell started it in 2019 to tell stories about the New York that works through the night.

We shoot on location across the five boroughs, hire crews from the neighborhoods we film in, and keep every production small enough to move by subway.
