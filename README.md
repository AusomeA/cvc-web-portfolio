# Website portfolio: Barbed Wire Glove Games LLC

Four concept websites for fictional small businesses, built as portfolio samples. Each one is built around a
tool its customers would actually use:

- **Juniper & Rye Bakehouse** (sourdough bakery): a live oven schedule driven by the visitor's clock, plus a
  custom-cake builder.
- **Summit Ridge Plumbing & Heating**: a "What's going on?" diagnosis that fills in a service ticket with the
  likely cause, usual price and urgency, plus a rate sheet and a coverage check.
- **Ironwood Strength Co.** (barbell gym): a plate loader that puts the right competition plates on a barbell,
  plus a class schedule with a live countdown.
- **Skip Stop Pictures** (film and TV production company, New York): a trailer-first homepage that opens like a
  film, with a reel that switches between four films, plus a merch shop with sizes and a working cart.

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks or build step (GSAP for some animation on two of the
sites). Photos are from Unsplash and Pexels under their free licenses; see each site's CREDITS.md. The businesses,
people, films, addresses and 555 phone numbers are made up.

The hub page (index.html) opens with the four sample sites running live in a laptop or phone frame, shows the tool
each one is built around on a phone, lets visitors pick their own kind of business to see the tool I'd build, and
lists the studio's shipped products.
