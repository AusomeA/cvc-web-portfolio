# Website portfolio: Barbed Wire Glove Games LLC

Three concept websites for fictional small businesses, built as portfolio samples. Each one is built around a
tool its customers would actually use:

- **Juniper & Rye Bakehouse** (sourdough bakery): a live oven schedule driven by the visitor's clock, plus a
  custom-cake builder.
- **Summit Ridge Plumbing & Heating**: a "What's going on?" diagnosis that fills in a service ticket with the
  likely cause, usual price and urgency, plus a rate sheet and a coverage check.
- **Ironwood Strength Co.** (barbell gym): a plate loader that puts the right competition plates on a barbell,
  plus a class schedule with a live countdown.

Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks or build step (GSAP for some animation). Photos are from
Unsplash and Pexels under their free licenses; see each site's CREDITS.md. The businesses, people, addresses and
555 phone numbers are made up.

Live: https://ausomea.github.io/cvc-web-portfolio/
