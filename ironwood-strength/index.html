    What we coach

    Every class has a coach on the floor and a cap of ten people, so someone is always watching your setup. The colors match the schedule below.

    • Strength Fundamentals

      New lifters welcome, 60 minutes

      Squat, bench press and deadlift, taught from the empty bar up. Start here if you've never lifted.

    • Powerlifting Prep

      Some experience, 75 minutes

      Programmed squat, bench and deadlift cycles aimed at a meet, a personal record or just a heavier bar.

    • Olympic Lifting Club

      Some experience, 75 minutes

      Snatch and clean and jerk, broken into drills you can repeat at home, on bumper plates.

    • Conditioning

      New lifters welcome, 45 minutes

      Sleds, kettlebells, rowers and carries. Hard work that scales to where you are this week.

    • Mobility and Recovery

      New lifters welcome, 45 minutes

      Led by our physical therapist. Hips, shoulders and ankles, so the bar ends up where it should.

    • Youth Athletics

      Ages 12 to 17, 60 minutes

      Lifting technique, sprinting and jumping for school athletes, with a parent welcome to watch.

    Your first class is free

    No card, no sales pitch. Pick a class and we'll save you a spot.

    1. Pick a class and a time

      Strength Fundamentals is the usual first class, but any class marked for new lifters works.

    2. Arrive ten minutes early

      A coach shows you around and watches you squat and hinge with an empty bar or a dowel.

    3. Train the class at your weights

      You'll leave knowing your starting numbers and which class to come back to.

      Questions

      Do I need to be in shape before I start?

      No. Strength Fundamentals starts with the empty bar, and coaches scale every movement to where you are. Most members couldn't deadlift their body weight when they joined.

      What should I bring to my first class?

      Flat shoes you can move in, a water bottle and clothes you can squat in. We have bars, plates, belts, chalk and a spare pair of lifting shoes in most sizes.

      Is the first class really free?

      Yes. One class in any format, and we don't ask for a card to book it.

      Can I come if I'm recovering from an injury?

      Usually. Tell us when you book and Sarah, our physical therapist, will spend a few minutes with you before class.

      Do you have a program for teenagers?

      Youth Athletics runs Monday and Wednesday at 4:30 PM for ages 12 to 17. Parents are welcome to watch.

      How do I cancel or pause?

      Tell the front desk or email us. Memberships are month to month, and we need 15 days' notice to pause or cancel.
