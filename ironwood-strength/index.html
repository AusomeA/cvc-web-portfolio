What We Coach

Class Types

Every class is coached, scaled, and capped in size — nobody trains alone in the corner.

Strength Fundamentals

Squat, bench, and deadlift technique in small groups. Built for first-timers and anyone rebuilding the basics.

Olympic Lifting Club

Snatch and clean-and-jerk technique work for intermediate and advanced lifters chasing new numbers.

Conditioning & Metcon

Kettlebells, sleds, and bodyweight circuits that scale from your first visit to competitive engine work.

Powerlifting Prep

Programmed strength cycles that build toward a meet, a PR, or just a heavier number on the bar.

Mobility & Recovery

Guided stretching, banded mobility, and recovery work led by our staff physical therapist.

Youth Athletics (12–17)

Real coaching on lifting fundamentals and athletic movement for young athletes, not a babysitting hour.

Questions

FAQ

Do I need experience to join?

No. Strength Fundamentals and Conditioning & Metcon are built for first-timers, and coaches scale every lift to your level.

What should I bring to my first class?

Closed-toe shoes, a water bottle, and clothes you can move in. We provide barbells, plates, kettlebells, and mats.

Is the first class really free?

Yes. New members get one free class in any format, no card required to try it.

Do you offer youth programs?

Yes. Youth Athletics runs Monday and Wednesday afternoons for ages 12–17.

Can I drop in without a membership?

Yes. Drop-In Passes are $25 per class, no membership required.

What's your membership policy?

Month-to-month, no long-term contract. Cancel or pause any plan with 15 days' notice.
