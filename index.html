Juniper & Rye Bakehouse

A sourdough bakery in Fort Collins, Colorado. Fictional business, working site.

View the live site

Bakery customers want to know when to come in, so the page opens on today’s oven schedule. It reads the visitor’s own clock and shows what’s in the oven, what comes out next and what’s usually gone by now, with a marker you can drag to plan a later visit.

Further down, a cake builder draws the cake in cross-section as you pick the size, sponge, filling and finish, prices it, and ends in a request form. It’s a demo, so nothing is sent.

Summit Ridge Plumbing & Heating

A residential plumbing and heating company in northern Colorado. Fictional business, working site.

View the live site

People land on a plumber’s site in the middle of a problem. The “What’s going on?” tool asks which fixture is acting up and what it’s doing, then fills in a service ticket: the likely cause, what it usually costs, how urgent it is and one next step. Emergencies turn the ticket red with the phone number at full size.

The rest is what a homeowner checks before calling: a rate sheet with weekday and after-hours prices, and a town or ZIP check that says whether the truck comes out to you.

Ironwood Strength Co.

A barbell gym in Greeley, Colorado. Fictional business, working site.

View the live site

The plate loader is a tool lifters actually use. Type a weight in kilograms or pounds and the right competition plates slide onto the bar, heaviest first, with the list for each side and the warm-up jumps worked out.

For people who haven’t lifted yet, there’s a weekly class schedule with filters and a live “next class starts in” line, and a free-class signup form. That form is a demo too.
