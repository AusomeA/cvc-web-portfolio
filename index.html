Trigonix

Color logic puzzle game for Android and iOS, built in Unreal Engine

I designed and built it, and I publish it. Players fill the board with one color, starting with 2 colors and working up to 12. It has Casual, Time Trial, Challenge and Multiplayer modes, 48 unlockable colors, metals and animated patterns, and in-app purchases.

My role: developer and publisher.

Trigonix on Google Play On the App Store

PoolKit

Performance plugin for Unreal Engine 5, in C++

A plugin for Unreal Engine 5.4 to 5.7 that reuses actors, widgets, sounds, Niagara effects, decals and meshes instead of spawning new ones. It works with no setup, sizes its pools automatically from real play, shows live graphs in a debugger window, supports multiplayer and has 27 automated tests. In the demo benchmark it cut game-thread time for projectiles by 6.1x.

My role: product owner and publisher (AI-assisted development).

PoolKit on itch.io

Chord Scribe

Chord chart app for musicians, on Android

Turns song lyrics into clean chord charts. Musicians drag chords onto words, change the key and capo, switch to Nashville numbers, view chord diagrams, add custom chords and section labels, and export to PDF. Published on Google Play by my studio, with a Pro subscription.

My role: studio owner and publisher (AI-assisted development).
